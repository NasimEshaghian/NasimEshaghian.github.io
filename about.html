About me

I’m an AI/ML engineer working on machine learning, language applications, and workflow automation. I enjoy understanding how a process works and finding practical ways to improve it.

What I work on

At Aqua Intelligent Technology and Pardazesh Seir Afagh, I develop and evaluate machine learning models for drinking-water treatment. My work includes preparing plant data, forecasting turbidity, supporting coagulant dosing decisions, and estimating chlorine demand and free chlorine residual.

At Hermes, I build workflows that connect business systems and reduce manual tasks. I work with n8n, PostgreSQL, Telegram bots, and APIs on CRM and exhibition-management processes.

My other projects include Persian speech recognition using Whisper and a pharmaceutical information assistant built with web crawling, semantic search, and RAG.

My background

I hold an M.Sc. in Information Security and co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on fake-news detection. My work has since moved toward applied AI and building tools around real data and everyday needs.

How I approach my work

I start by understanding the problem and the available data. Then I build, test, and improve the solution. I care about clear results, useful tools, and being honest about what a model can and cannot do.
