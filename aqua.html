Applied Industrial AI / Machine Learning

AI-Assisted Drinking-Water Treatment Decision Support

An ongoing applied machine-learning project exploring how operational water-quality data can support turbidity monitoring, coagulant scenario assessment, and chlorine-related decision support.

Ongoing Research & Development My contribution: AI/ML development, evaluation, and decision-support prototyping

Project Overview

This project explores a data-informed decision-support approach for drinking-water treatment operations. The work brings together data preparation, machine-learning experiments, time-aware evaluation, and an operator-oriented application prototype. Its purpose is to help make operational patterns easier to review—not to replace plant expertise or automate treatment decisions.

Water-quality monitoring

Preparing and analysing operational measurements to study changes in turbidity and identify patterns that may warrant attention.

Decision support

Evaluating candidate operating scenarios against water-quality objectives while accounting for the limits of historical evidence.

Model evaluation

Comparing candidate models with simple baselines and keeping model limitations visible throughout development.

Three Development Areas

The work is organised into three related development areas. Each is evaluated as a distinct problem because the targets, available evidence, and operational implications are different.

AREA 01

Turbidity Monitoring & Early Warning

Exploring approaches for identifying notable changes in raw-water turbidity and estimating the magnitude of potential events.

  • Data quality checks and temporal alignment.
  • Feature preparation from recent observations.
  • Separate classification and magnitude-estimation experiments.
  • Comparison with persistence-based baselines.
AREA 02

Coagulant Scenario Assessment

Studying whether historical process data can provide useful evidence when reviewing candidate coagulant operating scenarios.

  • Evaluation of candidate scenarios against a water-quality objective.
  • Use of model estimates alongside historical evidence.
  • Conservative handling when historical support is limited.
  • Operator review or abstention when evidence is insufficient.
AREA 03

Chlorine Demand & Residual Assessment

Investigating separate prediction tasks related to an operational chlorine-demand measure and free chlorine residual.

  • Preparation of historical and manually reviewed observations.
  • Chronological model evaluation.
  • Benchmarking candidate models against persistence.
  • Keeping candidates in evaluation until their reliability is demonstrated.

Development Workflow

A repeatable workflow is used to move from raw operational records to outputs that can be reviewed by a human operator.

Data review → Preparation & alignment → Feature engineering → Model experiments → Baseline evaluation → Operator review

Engineering & Evaluation Principles

Time-aware validation

Evaluation is designed to respect chronological order so that future observations do not leak into model training.

Baseline-first assessment

Candidate models are compared with simple reference approaches. Complexity alone is not treated as evidence of improvement.

Evidence-aware recommendations

Limited historical variation or insufficient support can lead to a cautious result or an explicit request for operator review.

Measured data remain distinct

Observed measurements and model predictions are treated as different information types and should be clearly distinguished in the interface.

Human-in-the-loop by design

The project is being developed as an advisory decision-support capability. It is not presented as an autonomous dosing controller and does not directly operate pumps, treatment equipment, or plant control systems.

Prototype & Technical Work

A Streamlit-based prototype has been developed to organise project outputs into operator-oriented workflows. The broader work includes data preparation, model experimentation, evaluation, configuration management, and application prototyping. Integration and validation remain part of the ongoing project.

Confidentiality & Project Status

This project is ongoing and involves non-public operational information. To respect confidentiality, this public overview intentionally excludes client and site identifiers, raw or identifiable plant data, sensor mappings, exact operating thresholds, chemical-dose settings, internal system details, and unreleased model results. The description is a high-level summary and should not be interpreted as evidence of production deployment or independently verified treatment outcomes.
